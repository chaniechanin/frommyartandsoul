The Artist

Chanie Chanin

I grew up in London and made my home in Crown Heights. I paint from a place of deep emotion and faith — the words we pray, the places we long for, and the light we reach toward.

Between raising my family, teaching art, and sharing my work through story and song, I've filled canvas after canvas with the things I believe most deeply.

“Every canvas is a story I couldn't say any other way.”
